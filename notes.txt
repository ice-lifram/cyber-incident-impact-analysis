Methods of Analysis
- Z-score
A Z-score quantifies the distance of a data point from the mean in terms of standard deviations. 

Z = (X - mu) / sigma
Where:

Z = Z-score
X = specific data point
μ (mu) = mean of the dataset
σ (sigma) = standard deviation of the dataset

If z-score is greater than 3 or less than -3

z > 2, unusual
z > 3, anomalous
